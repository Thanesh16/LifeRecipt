# 🌟 What is LifeReceipt?

LifeReceipt is an **AI-powered digital ownership intelligence platform**
designed to solve a simple but widespread problem:

> People purchase products, but the information required to manage those
> products throughout their lifetime becomes scattered everywhere.

A purchase may generate:

- 🧾 Physical receipts
- 📄 PDF invoices
- 📧 Email confirmations
- 🛡️ Warranty information
- 🔧 Service records
- 🧰 Maintenance records
- 📚 Product manuals
- 💰 Repair expenses
- 🔄 Ownership transfer information

LifeReceipt brings these pieces together into a **single digital ownership
record**.

The goal is not simply to store receipts.

The goal is to understand:

> **What do I own?  
> When did I purchase it?  
> How much did it cost?  
> Is it still under warranty?  
> What service has it received?  
> What documents belong to it?  
> How much has it cost me overall?  
> Can I safely transfer its ownership?  
> What should I do next?**

# 🎯 The Problem

## 1. Receipts are fragmented

Purchase information may exist in:

- Paper receipts
- PDF invoices
- Email inboxes
- Retailer portals
- Physical folders
- Messaging applications
- Scanned documents

Finding the correct document months later can be difficult.

---

## 2. Warranty information gets forgotten

Users often don't remember:

- Purchase dates
- Warranty duration
- Warranty expiry dates
- Return deadlines
- Required claim documents

As a result, users may miss opportunities that were available to them.

---

## 3. Product service history is disconnected

A product may have undergone several:

- Repairs
- Maintenance visits
- Part replacements
- Service-center visits

but these events are rarely maintained as one continuous ownership history.

---

## 4. Ownership transfer is difficult

When selling a product, the buyer may want evidence of:

- Purchase
- Genuine ownership
- Warranty
- Service history
- Product documents

This information is usually spread across multiple files.

---

## 5. Users don't know the total cost of ownership

The purchase price is only the beginning.

A product may later generate:

+

+
Maintenance
+
Accessories

Actual Ownership Cost
